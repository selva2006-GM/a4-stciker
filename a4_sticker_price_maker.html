A4 Sticker Price Printer

Enter prices individually or fill batches of stickers with the same price.

Fill multiple stickers at once

Example: quantity 23, price 99 fills the next 23 stickers with ₹99. Add another batch for quantity 12 at ₹45.

Batches fill from the first sticker onward, in reading order (left to right, then top to bottom). Remaining stickers are left blank. Maximum 84 stickers.
